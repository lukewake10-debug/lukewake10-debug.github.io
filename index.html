PAC OpenClaw

PAC OpenClaw is a private personal and household assistant system operated by its account owner. It is being prepared to continue existing personal workflows through PAC, Coach and Roo. Buzz remains paused and has no Google workflow enabled. This site provides information; it has no public registration, login, uploads or assistant interface.

Connection and feature status

At preparation on 8 November 2026, an OAuth client had been created and stored privately, with no Google account grant made through it. The first connection is limited to account-holder sign-in, consent, encrypted credential storage and checking the authorised account identity. It does not activate email reading, calendar or document changes, AI processing, bot messages or routines.

The installed Google command-line client contains email, calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets operations. After the relevant tests and approvals, the intended workflows are personal email and follow-ups; the Family calendar; existing authorised knowledge and relocation records; and Coach's existing food diary and supplied food/activity records. These are existing workflow requirements, not a claim that the new assistant's integrations and safeguards are all deployed.

Google's app publishing status and the private assistant's production status are separate. Moving the OAuth app out of Testing would not switch on OpenClaw, grant access to another person, or guarantee that refresh access lasts forever.
