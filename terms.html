PAC OpenClaw terms draft

Prepared 8 November 2026 for review. PAC OpenClaw is being prepared as a private personal and household assistant. It is not offered as a public registration service.

Only accounts individually authorised by their holders and approved by the operator may connect. An account connection does not grant unrestricted permission to send messages, change records, delete data or incur costs. Those actions must follow the applicable approval rules.

Google connections and production routines remain inactive during preparation. This information site does not provide access to the private assistant or grant any access to a Google account.

Google, OpenAI, Telegram and GitHub remain separate services with their own applicable terms and account controls. No purchase or payment can be made through these information pages.
