PAC OpenClaw terms of use

Updated 8 November 2026. PAC OpenClaw is a private personal/household assistant being prepared by its operator, not a public registration service. Its information website provides no assistant login, account connection form or payment facility.

Only individually authorised accounts may be provisioned locally. Google app publishing, account consent, feature activation and production cutover are separate decisions. An In-production Google OAuth app does not mean the private assistant is in production or that refresh access can never expire or be revoked.

The proposed first Google connection covers the operator's own account identity, consent and encrypted credential storage only. It does not approve mailbox/file/event access, writes, external sends, AI processing, bot messages, paid calls, billing changes or routine activation. Another household account needs its own appropriate owner authorisation. NHSmail is excluded; Buzz stays paused.

Later use must follow the existing task, resource and approval boundaries. OAuth rights can exceed those boundaries. Exact external sends require their applicable approval; mailbox deletion/trashing is forbidden; Family-calendar and specific document/spreadsheet/folder restrictions must be enforced and tested before assistant activation. Existing full-migration requirements are not replaced by a narrower connection test.

Users should review the privacy notice, including storage, future AI transfers, training-control limits, retention and deletion. Google, OpenAI, Telegram, OVHcloud and GitHub are separate services with their own applicable terms and controls. This notice does not promise zero retention, unlimited availability, a complete backup or legal/compliance certification.

Account holders can withdraw access and request local-data deletion through the operator's existing private contact channel. Do not publish sensitive information in a GitHub issue. Revoking an OAuth grant and deleting retained copies are different actions, with the limits described in the privacy notice.
