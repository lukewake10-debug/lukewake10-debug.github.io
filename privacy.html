PAC OpenClaw privacy policy draft

Prepared 8 November 2026 for review. Google account connections for this app are not active. Operational data-handling details will be finalised and disclosed before account authorisation.

This information site

These pages contain general information about PAC OpenClaw. They have no login, upload forms or operator-added analytics. Private emails, documents, assistant memories and credentials will not be published here. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Google account information

No Google OAuth client or account grant has been created for this app at the time of this draft. The app cannot currently use its own Google connection to read or change an account.

The planned service would request only the permissions needed for approved email, calendar and document features. Before connection, each account holder must receive a clear description of the requested data, permitted actions, any relevant transfer to an AI provider, private storage, retention and deletion arrangements. The account holder must consent to the actual access. The final operational policy must reflect the implemented controls before any Google data flows through this connection.

Google account data will not be used for advertising, resale or general AI model training. The public information site is separate from the private assistant's data storage.

Choices and contact

Account holders can withdraw an app's Google access through their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Withdrawal and deletion of already retained assistant records are separate actions; those procedures must be documented before activation. Authorised household users should contact the operator through the private channel used to arrange their access. Do not post account details or private records in a public GitHub issue.
